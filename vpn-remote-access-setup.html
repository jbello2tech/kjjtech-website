FAQ

VPN & remote access questions

Which VPN do you recommend on UniFi?

WireGuard for most setups: it's fast, built into UniFi gateways, and reconnects quickly when you change networks. Teleport is great when your internet provider blocks incoming connections or you want one-click setup on phones and laptops.

Can you set up a VPN remotely?

Yes. Most VPN work is done remotely, anywhere in the U.S. We just need access to your UniFi console. Onsite help is available around Orlando.

Can I make my traffic show my home IP while traveling?

Yes, with a home VPN back to your own router, ideally through a travel router so every device is covered. Our home VPN guide walks through it, or we can set it up for you.

Is port forwarding a safe way to access my cameras or NAS remotely?

Generally no. Port forwarding exposes those devices to the whole internet. A VPN keeps them private and only lets in people with a VPN profile.
