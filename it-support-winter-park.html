Why local matters

Networks built for Winter Park homes and businesses

Older Homes, Thick Walls

Many Winter Park homes are older or have been renovated over decades. Plaster walls (some with metal lath behind them), concrete block and additions built at different times all block Wi-Fi in different ways. We plan access points around how your house is actually built, wiring them back where we can instead of relying on mesh.

Shops, Studios & Offices

Retail, salons, restaurants and professional offices near Park Avenue and along Fairbanks and Aloma need separate guest and staff Wi-Fi, reliable card terminals and cameras that work. We set it up once, properly, and document it.

Home Offices

If you work from home, your network is your office. We separate work devices from the family's and IoT gadgets, and add a backup internet connection if you can't afford to be offline.

Security Cameras

UniFi Protect cameras with local recording and no monthly cloud fee, managed in the same app as your Wi-Fi. See security camera installation.

Storm & Power Readiness

Central Florida gets a lot of lightning and summer storms. A small battery backup (UPS) and surge protection on your modem, router and switch keep a brief flicker from taking your network down or damaging equipment.

FAQ

Winter Park IT questions

Do you come onsite in Winter Park?

Yes. Winter Park is in our regular onsite area, with no travel fee. Onsite visits start at a 2-hour block.

Why is my Wi-Fi bad in some rooms of my house?

Usually it's walls and placement: plaster, metal lath and concrete block absorb Wi-Fi, and a single router in a corner can't reach the far side of the house. The fix is usually one or two well-placed, wired access points. Our AP placement guide explains how we plan it.

Do you work with businesses or just homes?

Both. We support homes and home offices, plus retail, restaurants, studios and professional offices.

How much does it cost?

Hourly from $200, with discounted 2- and 5-hour blocks. Hardware is extra. See pricing, or start with a free 30-minute call.

Can you fix a UniFi system someone else installed?

Yes. We audit, re-tune and clean up existing UniFi installs, whether you set it up yourself or inherited it.
