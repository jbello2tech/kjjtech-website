Recent work

Fixing the problem, not the symptom

Tin Roof Orlando: Wi-Fi that kept failing a busy I-Drive venue

Tin Roof is a live-music bar and restaurant at Icon Park on International Drive, with bands playing nightly. Their Wi-Fi was severely unreliable, and the network had gone a long time without anyone looking after it.

Adding more access points or blaming the internet provider would have been the easy answer. Instead, we went through the network piece by piece and found the real cause: every access point was cabled, but all of them were meshing wirelessly to each other instead of using their wired uplinks. Each AP was squeezing its traffic through a weak wireless link while a perfectly good cable sat unused.

We got every AP running on its wired connection as designed and cleaned up the neglected configuration, so each one now has its full wired bandwidth for staff and guests.

Outside Orlando, we've designed and built complete UniFi networks with VLANs and cameras for A2F Studios in Miami and Weather Tite Windows in Tampa Bay, including UniFi Talk phones for Weather Tite. See those projects on our UniFi installation page.
