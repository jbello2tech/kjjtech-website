What's covered

Everything a small business network needs

Proactive Monitoring

We're alerted when an internet line, switch, access point or camera goes offline, often before anyone in the office notices, and start working on it right away.

Updates & Patching

Firmware updates for your gateway, switches and access points, tested and scheduled outside business hours so updates don't become outages.

Security Reviews

Regular checks of firewall rules, remote access, admin accounts and old port forwards, the gaps that quietly open up over time.

A Real Person to Call

A dedicated technician who knows your network. No call centers and no explaining your setup from scratch every time.

Documentation

An up-to-date record of your network: equipment, passwords handled securely, IP plan and configuration, so nothing depends on one person's memory.

Quarterly Reviews

A short review each quarter: what happened, what we fixed, what's aging out and what to budget for next, in plain English.

FAQ

Managed support questions

How much does managed IT support cost?

It depends on the number of locations, devices and users, so we quote it after a free assessment. If you'd rather pay as you go, our hourly rate and prepaid blocks are listed on the pricing page.

Do you only support UniFi networks?

UniFi is our specialty, but we support the whole office network, including internet connections, firewalls, Wi-Fi, cameras and the devices on it. If a change of equipment would save you money or headaches, we'll tell you.

Do you offer managed support outside Orlando?

Yes. Monitoring, updates and most fixes are done remotely, so we support businesses anywhere in the U.S. Onsite work is available around Orlando, and we coordinate with a local technician elsewhere.

Is there a long-term contract?

We'll go over terms during the assessment. Our goal is to keep you because the network runs well, not because of a contract.

What if we already have someone for IT?

We often work alongside an in-house IT person or a general IT provider, handling the network side while they handle computers and software.
