Details

The fine print (it's short)

Who can I refer?

Any business or homeowner who isn't already a KJJ Tech client: onsite in the Orlando area or remote anywhere in the U.S.

Is there a limit?

No. Every new client you refer earns you 10% off a future job.

When does the discount apply?

The new client's 10% applies to their first job. Yours applies to your next job after their first job is paid. Discounts apply to one job each and can't be combined with other offers.

Do I need to be a current client?

You need to have worked with us at least once. That's what makes your recommendation count.
