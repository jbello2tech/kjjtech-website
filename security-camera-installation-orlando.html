FAQ

Security camera questions

Is there a monthly fee for UniFi Protect cameras?

No. UniFi Protect records to hardware at your location, so there's no per-camera cloud subscription. You buy the equipment once.

Where is the video stored?

On a hard drive in a UniFi recorder (NVR) or a UniFi gateway that supports Protect, at your home or business. How many days you can keep depends on the number of cameras, their resolution and the drive size, and we size it for you.

Can I watch my cameras from my phone?

Yes. The UniFi Protect app shows live view, alerts and recorded footage from anywhere.

Do the cameras need power outlets?

No. UniFi cameras are powered over the same network cable that carries the video (PoE), so each camera needs just one cable.

Can you add cameras to the UniFi network I already have?

Usually, yes. If you already have a UniFi gateway with storage support, cameras can often be added to it. We'll check your setup on the free call. See our UniFi installation page for the rest of the stack.

How much does camera installation cost?

Labor is billed hourly from $200/hr, with discounted 2- and 5-hour blocks, and onsite visits within 30 miles of Orlando start at a 2-hour block. Hardware is extra, and we estimate the time on the free call before anything is scheduled. See pricing.
