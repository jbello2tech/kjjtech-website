What we install

The full UniFi stack

Wi-Fi Access Points

Wi-Fi that reaches every room. We plan access point locations from your floor plan, mount them on the ceiling where they work best, and tune them so phones and laptops switch smoothly as you move around.

UniFi Gateways

The brain of your network: your router and firewall in one. We set up your UniFi gateway, a backup internet connection if you have one, and secure remote access.

Switches & PoE

The wired backbone. We size switches that also power your access points and cameras through the network cable (PoE), so you don't need extra outlets on the ceiling.

Separate Networks & Firewall

Keep guests, smart devices and cameras off the network your computers use. Each group gets its own network (VLAN), protected by a properly configured zone-based firewall.

UniFi Protect Cameras

Security cameras that record to a recorder at your location (NVR), managed in the same UniFi app as your Wi-Fi. No separate vendor and no monthly cloud subscription.

Fix an Existing Setup

Already have UniFi but it's not performing? We audit, re-tune and clean up DIY or inherited installs.

Recent projects

UniFi networks we've built and fixed

Orlando · Live-music venue

Tin Roof Orlando

The problem: Wi-Fi across this busy I-Drive bar and music venue was severely unreliable, and the network had gone without care for a long time.

What we found: every access point was cabled, yet all of them were meshing wirelessly to each other instead of using their wired uplinks, so each AP was sharing a weak wireless backhaul.

The fix: we got every AP running on its wired connection as designed and cleaned up the configuration, giving each one its full wired bandwidth.

Miami · Recording studio

A2F Studios

The problem: everything, from studio machines to guests and devices, sat on a single flat network.

What we did: designed the entire network, installed it onsite and configured it remotely. We split traffic into VLANs, applied networking best practices and enabled UniFi's security protections.

Beyond Wi-Fi: UniFi Protect cameras, managed from the same system as the network.

Tampa Bay · Window & door company

Weather Tite Windows

The problem: an aging network that needed a full revamp.

What we did: helped choose the right hardware before purchase, then rebuilt the network from scratch on new UniFi equipment with VLANs and best-practice configuration, installed onsite and configured remotely.

Beyond Wi-Fi: UniFi Protect cameras and UniFi Talk phones, with ongoing post-sale support.

FAQ

UniFi installation questions

Do you install UniFi for homes or just businesses?

Both. We set up UniFi for homeowners who want reliable whole-home Wi-Fi as well as offices, retail and multi-site businesses.

How many access points will I need?

It depends on square footage, walls and how many devices you have. As a rough guide, a 1,500-3,000 sq ft home usually needs two, and an office needs about one per 1,000-1,500 sq ft. Our AP placement guide goes into detail, or we'll plan it for you from your floor plan.

Can I buy the equipment myself?

Yes. We'll give you a parts list to order, or help you source it. Either way, we'll make sure you're not overbuying.

How much does installation cost?

Labor is billed hourly from $200/hr, with discounted 2- and 5-hour blocks. Onsite visits within 30 miles of Orlando require a 2-hour minimum (one 2-hour block). Beyond 30 miles, travel is quoted before booking. We'll estimate the time on the free call before anything is scheduled. See the pricing page, or our guide to what UniFi installation costs for example budgets.

Should I choose UniFi or Meraki?

For most small businesses and homes, UniFi delivers the same core features with no license fees. We compare them in detail in UniFi vs Meraki.

Not in Orlando?

We plan and configure UniFi networks remotely anywhere in the U.S. Your local installer mounts the hardware and we handle the design and configuration.
