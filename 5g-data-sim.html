FAQ

5G data SIM questions

Is it really unlimited?

Yes. There's no data cap, no built-in speed limit after a certain amount, and no overage charge. Normal acceptable-use rules apply, as with any carrier.

How much does it cost?

$149 per month, month-to-month, or $1,440 when you pay for a year up front, which works out to $120 per month and saves $348. There's no activation fee, and no sales tax is added. A static IP is an optional add-on for $10/month ($120/year on the yearly plan).

Do I get a public IP address?

In most locations, yes: a public IPv4 address instead of the carrier-grade NAT most cellular plans use. It's typical but not guaranteed, and it can change. If you need a fixed, reachable address, add a static IP.

Do I need a static IP?

Not for travel with a Mudi 7, since your router connects out to your home VPN. Consider one if the 5G connection is hosting something, like a VPN server or camera access, or if you need to be on an allowlist.

Which routers does it work with?

The GL.iNet Mudi 7, the UniFi 5G Max (indoor and outdoor), and most other unlocked 5G routers and hotspots that take a nano-SIM, including other GL.iNet and Peplink models. Need a router? We sell complete kits with the SIM, set up and ready to go.

Where does it work?

On the T-Mobile 5G and 4G network across the lower 48 states, Hawaii and Puerto Rico. It doesn't work in Alaska, Canada, Mexico or internationally.

How do I update my card or cancel?

Use the customer portal with the email you ordered with. You can update your card, download receipts or cancel; cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period.

Can I put it in my phone?

No. It's a data-only SIM for routers and hotspots, with no calling, texting or phone number.
